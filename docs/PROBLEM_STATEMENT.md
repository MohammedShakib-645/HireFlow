# Problem Statement

## Title

**HireFlow — AI-Powered Recruitment Intelligence Agent**

## Problem

Modern recruitment requires recruiters to evaluate information from multiple sources: job descriptions, resumes, project claims, education, work experience, interviews, and recruiter notes.

The challenge is not simply finding candidate information. The challenge is turning scattered information into **clear, traceable and decision-supporting evidence**.

## Key Challenges

### 1. Manual Screening

Recruiters can spend significant time reading and comparing resumes against job requirements.

### 2. Scattered Candidate Information

Relevant evidence can be distributed across resumes, projects, documents, interview responses and notes.

### 3. Requirement Matching

A candidate may mention a skill without providing enough evidence of practical experience.

### 4. Evidence Gaps

Important requirements may be missing, weakly supported, or unclear.

### 5. Interview Preparation

Recruiters need useful questions that explore both known strengths and uncertain areas.

### 6. Interview Evidence

Interview answers can reveal information that changes the understanding of a candidate's profile.

### 7. Traceability

Recruiters need to understand where an insight came from instead of receiving unexplained AI conclusions.

## Core Problem

> **More candidates. More data. Less clarity.**

HireFlow addresses this problem by creating an evidence-first, agentic workflow that connects job requirements, candidate evidence and interview intelligence.

## Design Constraint

HireFlow is designed as a **decision-support system**.

It does not claim that an AI agent should make the final hiring decision.

**AI supports the decision. The human decides.**
