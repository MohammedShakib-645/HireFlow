# Solution Overview

## HireFlow

**AI-Powered Recruitment Intelligence Agent**

### Tagline

**Smarter Hiring. Better Decisions.**

**Not just resumes. Real evidence.**

## Solution

HireFlow transforms recruitment analysis into a structured agentic pipeline:

**Job Requirements**  
↓  
**Candidate Evidence**  
↓  
**AI Analysis**  
↓  
**Evidence Mapping**  
↓  
**Gap Detection**  
↓  
**Adaptive Interview**  
↓  
**Interview Analysis**  
↓  
**Recruiter Insights**  
↓  
**Human Decision**

## Core Modules

### JD Analyzer Agent

Extracts:

- Skills
- Experience requirements
- Role responsibilities
- Education requirements
- Other relevant criteria

### Resume Analyzer

Extracts and structures:

- Skills
- Projects
- Education
- Experience
- Candidate claims

### Evidence Mapping

Connects each job requirement to available candidate evidence.

Example:

**Requirement:** Python  
**Evidence:** Candidate project + resume  
**Status:** VERIFIED

### Gap Detection

Flags information that is:

- Missing
- Weakly supported
- Unclear
- Requiring validation

### AI Interview Agent

Generates interview questions based on:

- Job requirements
- Candidate evidence
- Evidence gaps
- Previous responses

### Interview Analysis

Analyzes responses for:

- Evidence
- Strengths
- Gaps
- New information
- Uncertainty

### Evaluation Agent

Produces a structured evidence view rather than an unexplained score.

### Recruiter Report

Provides:

- Requirement coverage
- Evidence references
- Strengths
- Gaps
- Uncertainty
- Interview insights
- Sources

## Human-in-the-Loop

HireFlow keeps the recruiter involved throughout the process.

> **AI supports the decision. The human decides.**

## What Makes HireFlow Different

### Evidence-First

Insights trace back to candidate evidence.

### Adaptive Interview

Questions evolve based on what is known and unknown.

### Uncertainty Detection

Missing information is flagged instead of being treated as verified.

### Auditability

Recruiters can trace where insights came from.

### Human-in-the-Loop

AI assists the workflow while the recruiter retains final decision authority.
